## Professional Life

> Systems, platforms, and infrastructure, oh my!

In my professional career, I manage the automation, deployment, operation, and change processes
of various software solutions and platforms in enterprise environments.
Infrastructure/DevOps Engineer is the best title I could come up with to describe that,
but I've also been called a Platform Engineer, Site Reliability Engineer, IT Operations Specialist,
and probably some others as well.

My professional experience has been within the financial sector,
either working for a payment gateway (Tempus Technologies) or a bank (PNC),
so I'm used to working within heavily regulated environments.

### Specializations

I mostly specialize in using Ansible for infrastructure-as-code automation. I have authored two Ansible modules:
[win_certificate_info](https://docs.ansible.com/projects/ansible/latest/collections/ansible/windows/win_certificate_info_module.html), and
[win_hosts](https://docs.ansible.com/projects/ansible/latest/collections/ansible/windows/win_hosts_module.html).

I also have extensive experience in PowerShell. It is probably my most comfortable language to work in,
as I have created many modules using this language to help automate infrastructure on Windows Servers.
Most of that work was custom solutions for an employer, but [wimau](https://github.com/mhunsber/wimau)
is an example of a module I have published on the public PowerShell Gallery.

Currently, I am focused on managing the Elastic Stack platform (Elasticsearch, Logstash, and Kibana) for observability, logging, and analytics.
